WHAT IS WABBASEBALL?

A retro baseball game built on real numbers. Every big leaguer from 2015 to 2026 gets a card rated from what he actually did that season. Then it's your call: manage every pitch, grab a bat when it matters, or sim straight to October.

  • 30 clubs
  • 12 seasons
  • 11,900+ player cards
  • 30 real parks

FOUR WAYS TO PLAY

QUICK PLAY

One game, any two clubs.

Any club from any season since 2015, head to head. The game sizes up the matchup before the first pitch.

FRANCHISE

Run a club season after season.

Start in any year from 2015 to 2026. Lineups, trades, contracts, the draft and the payroll are all yours.

BATTLEWORLD BASEBALL

Ten challenges, every season since 2015.

Build a roster from any player in any season, then win a league under one rule: one season only, nobody over 85, lefties only, guys from losing teams.

HOMERUN DERBY

Eight sluggers, one night.

Pick your eight, swing for the seats. It's in the works.

EVERY PLAYER GETS A CARD

Rated from what he did that season, with no ceiling: 99 is an MVP year. Every season has its own card design, and the badge tells you the tier, from Dirt all the way up to Arkenstone.

Game day

PLAY IT YOUR WAY

MANAGE, PLAY, OR SIMULATE

Run it from the dugout, step in for one at-bat, or sim to the ninth. Switch any time.

BAT AND PITCH YOURSELF

Your timing moves the odds. Their ratings set the ceiling. A .220 hitter won't turn into Judge just because you're good at this.

REAL PARKS, REAL WALLS

Every park is built to its real dimensions. The short porch in the Bronx, the Monster in Boston, the ivy at Wrigley.

EVERY GAME IS A BROADCAST

Starting pitcher matchups, a live score bug, replays from the camera that caught it.

THE DAILY DINGER

Your league gets its own paper. Headlines, standings, the bracket, and a crossword nobody asked for.
